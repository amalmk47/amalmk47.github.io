Publications & Research

Enhanced Electric Vehicle Energy Consumption Prediction Using TabTransformer, TabNet, and BERT Embeddings

IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 2025, Vol. 21, pp. 7445–7454 (IF: 9.9, CiteScore: 22.5)

Developed deep learning models (TabTransformer, TabNet) and BERT-based feature embeddings to optimize EV energy consumption prediction. Achieved MAE of 0.38 with a 20% accuracy improvement using LightGBM and KMeans-based clustering, with hyperparameters tuned via Bayesian optimization (Optuna).

Tech Stack: Python, TabNet, TabTransformer, LightGBM, BERT, Optuna, Scikit-learn
